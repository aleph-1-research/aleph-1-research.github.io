### Set and Setting

Drugs are mysterious creatures.  When we consume a drug there is a tendency to believe that a because the drug is the same, the experience will be the same.  But you are different.  You're body, you're mind, and location in space is all different.  Drugs interact with you as are you in this ever changing moment.  So, when doing drugs it is helpful to start by attending yourself, your body, your mind, how you feel, and where you are in space.  Do you feel safe?  All of this will change your experience.

### Dosage

### Testing

Test for:
- The presence of the drug you're looking for
- The presence of the drugs you're not looking for
- Concentration of the drug you're looking for if possible
### Harm Reduction

Complex topic. Every drug effects the body in different ways. Attend the ways its effecting you.  

Work with your body.  Some methods of ingestions don't work for some bodies, so find alternatives.
