#### Recipe

Equal parts Yerba Mate, Chocolate, and Lemon Balm.  Combine in jar with at least 1/3 of the jar empty to allow for enough vegetable glycerin. Fill container with vegetable glycerin. Let sit for a month in a cool, dark area.  Give the jar a shake over day or so. Infuse for a month.  Filter through a coffee strainer and bottle in a glass bottle with a eye dropper.
