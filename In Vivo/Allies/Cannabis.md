Amongst my oldest friends, Cannabis is a truly unique plant.  Containing numerous psychoactive cannabinoids including Tetrahydrocannabinol (THC), cannabidol (CBD) and at least 83 others.[^1]

### Ingestion Methods

Cannabis is typically smoked or eaten.  I personally prefer to vaporize this plant.  It is my belief that you actually end up destroying a considerable amount of the active chemicals when smoking, but vaporizing will allow you to fully extract it's various chemicals.  Depending on the effects you desire, you can vaporize cannabis at:

- 157-165°C - This temperature will give you very little THC, but highlight the flavor of the terpenes. 
- 165-199°C - At this temperature you will start getting most cannabinoids including THC and CBD. The flavor will be richer and the vapor will be denser.  
- 199-215°C - The final cannabinoids will be vaporized at these temperatures including CBN and THCV.  The vapor from this temperature tends to induce more of a body high and sedative effects. 

Concentrates in the form of hash, rosin, and others have also become quite popular these days.  I tend to avoid them as I have found that they tend to spike my tolerance very quickly, and cannabis becomes a less enjoyable and more expensive activity.  The cartridge is a particular problem for me, as it is so convenient that I end up doing it all the time.  In most cases I don't experience compulsive redosing with cannabis, but with cartridges I definitely do.  So I stay away from them as best I can. 

When eating Cannabis, the plant material needs to be heated in order decarboxylate THCA into THC and CBDA into CBD.[^2]  This is typically done either directly in the oven or using the double boiler method when making an infused butter or oil.  When prepared at home exercise caution when consuming. It can be very hard to determine the precise dosage of the product you produce, and I have had friends get much higher than they desired by consuming home made infusions.  Store bought is, of course, much easier, and will provide a reliable dosage. 
### Dosage

Really depends on your tolerance.  I typically consume ~0.25-0.5g  when vaporizing. When smoking I will typically be at a higher dosage, 0.5-1g. 

As is usually the case, if you don't consume this plant regularly start small. A hit or two will probably do you just fine. 
### Effects

The effects of this chemical vary widely and are heavily impacted by your tolerance and dosage.  

At lower dosages there is a fully body sensation. I become very relaxed and inclined to sit and enjoy content.  It tends to offer some relief from anxiety.  Good for sexual activity. Puts me to sleep typically within a couple hours of consuming.

At higher dosages the psychedelic aspects of this plant become apparent.  I have had very unique and interesting close eyed visuals while on cannabis alone, though this tends to only happen when I've taken considerable time off and my tolerance is close to baseline. 
### Harm Reduction

Smoking anything is not terribly good for you.  I recommend vaporizing if you consume cannabis regularly. 

I have frequently heard the myth that this plant is not addictive. I can verify from experience that this is not true.  As a chemical which induces pleasure in the body, you are very capable of forming habitual and even compulsive patterns with it.  I have had periods of my life where I have consumed as much as an ounce a week. 

It has taken a lot of work, but with this particular plant I currently apply the following system to manage my desire for it. I buy 3.5g when there is a specific event I know I will want cannabis for. I then allow myself to consume it, though I do my best to constrain my use to the evenings because it puts me to sleep and makes my days much less productive. When it runs out, I wait to get more until there is an event that I'm again excited to have it for. 

### Interactions

I love the interaction between cannabis and [[Allies/LSD|LSD]]. I tend to wait until I've passed through the peak of the acid experience, and then consume cannabis to induce interesting visuals and bodily sensations.

A couple notes on this interaction.  Consuming cannabis prior to LSD, in my experience, tends to weaken the acid experience. I am unsure if this is some kind of cross-tolerance or some other effect, but if you want to maximize your acid experience then I would wait to consume the cannabis until after you've taken the acid.
### Experiments

One of my most difficult psychedelic experiences was caused by the combination of [[Allies/LSD|LSD]], cannabis and [[Allies/Nitrous Oxide|Nitrous Oxide]]. I had taken a couple month tolerance break, and one Saturday I decided to indulge in some of my favorite chemicals at the time.  While peaking on acid I smoked a joint and did a handful of whippits. 

Very quickly I realized that something had gone wrong. My perception of sound became quite distorted, and an intense feeling within my awareness built until it became quite painful.  My mind became fixated on the idea that I had been poisoned. This experience persisted for a couple hours, which I spent pacing and laying in a low sensory environment.  When the experience finally broke, i was rewarded with a vision of the secret. But I'd say that particular experience was probably not worth the pain.
