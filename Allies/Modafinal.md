##### Experiments

1/2 tablet oral - Took the half tablet of the R isomer (Armodafinil) within about an hour of waking up.  The experience was quite perceptible and similar to my experience with Adderall. I felt stimulated, a surge of energy in my heart present for an extended part of the peak.  I waited until I was past the peak to indulge in some cocaine and ketamine in a roughly 1:3 ratio via boofing.  They all played quite nicely together, and felt happily stimulated and capable of interesting movement.  The idea for this book arose during this very experience.
