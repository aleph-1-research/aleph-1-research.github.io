##### Harm Raeduction

Very limited information is available on the potential impacts of this chemical.  Given its similarity to MDMA my intuition is that one would risk overtaxing the serotonin system if done frequently, so personally I will treat it like that and not do this chemical frequency.  Probably for the best anyway, it's quite expensive.

I did not find that I experienced intense desire for this drug in the same way that I do with cocaine or 3-MMC, so I do not believe that this drug induces compulsive redosing.  
##### Experiments

200 mg, oral - I prepared the dose in a gel capsule for myself and my partner. As this was the first time we tried this chemical, we started by ingesting a small part of the chemical by opening the capsule and putting some directly on the tongue. We then waited ten minutes to ensure that neither of us were hypersensitive to it.

After ten minutes had passed, we began to feel the stimulant express ourself in our bodies.  Feeling confident that we weren't hypersensitive, we proceeded to empty the gel capsules into our mouths and swallowed the empty capsules. The flavor was similar but different to MDMA.

We then went about our planned activities as we waited for the chemical to fully kick in.  Another ten minutes later or so there was an intense, rapid onset of the chemical. Very reminiscent of MDMA.  Heart full. Sensations heightened. Our bodies already filled with energy from the LSD we had taken three hours earlier, engaged in their activities with great enthusiasm. 

After some time we danced in our living room.  Despite having already spent hours engaged in amorous activities, it only took a few minutes dancing for arousal to express itself yet again.  This is a very horny drug indeed.

Later in the experience we engaged in two conversations of a more difficult nature.  Hurt from the past presented itself as we talked, and we explored the feeling seeking its name and its root. It's hard to piece apart what elements of our conversation were supported by our existing ability to work with feelings, the LSD, and then this chemical.  But as we explored the feelings, we were able to understand their nature, what they were telling us, and process them productively.

About two hours after ingesting this chemical we layered on some ketamine.  I boofed roughly 200 mg. The effects of ketamine were somewhat muted, or rather changed... Not unlike the combination of ketamine with other stimulants like cocaine or 3-MMC, the effects filled the body with pleasure but I did not experience the reduced mobility in the way one does on ketamine alone.  Instead, I pulled out my favorite flow toy, put on some music, and played in the living room.  My body felt incredible as it moved, and I knew that I need to practice restraint as I did not feel the wear on my body as I moved in the way I do at groundstate.  Very easy to over do it. 

Two days after both I and my partner noticed that we were irritable in a way that was a little unusual.  Perhaps the consequence of this drug, though we had both been burning the candle at both ends for some time...
