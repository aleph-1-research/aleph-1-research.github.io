This book is an accumulation of research that I have conducted into chemicals which interact with the body in various ways.  This ranges from plants which contains various alkaloids and other compounds that interact with the body as well as refined and synthesized chemicals.  I have found in my research that there is a great number of plants and chemicals that you can experiment with, but the overwhelming number makes it hard to know which are the ones to try.  Over my life I have leveraged the recommendations of my friends who are similarly interested in functional medicine and psychopharmacology to identify potential allies that I might explore.

A warning.  Science is inherently risky. When you make your own body the laboratory, you are accepting certain risks.  Do not take my words, written here, as a guide for what you can do safely. Our bodies are different.  This work documents my research with my body, not extensive studies into the effects of the drugs on many bodies.  The dosages, experiences, and recommendations you will find in here all come with the caveat that they have worked for me... but you are different.  Milage may vary.  Be careful.
